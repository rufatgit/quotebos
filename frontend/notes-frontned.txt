Explore A discovery feed — browsable quotes sorted by trending, newest, most saved, 
or staff picks. Think infinite scroll with filter chips for mood or length.
 Essentially the homepage's "Recent Quotes" section expanded into a full browsable library.

Authors A directory of all authors — searchable, filterable by era (Ancient, Medieval, Modern, Contemporary) 
or field (Philosophy, Literature, Politics, Science). Each author gets a profile page with their bio,
 all their quotes, and related authors.

Collections Curated sets of quotes grouped around a theme — "Quotes for Monday mornings,"
 "What great writers said about death," "The Stoics on anger." Think of it like playlists.
  Could be editor-curated or user-created (if there's an account system).
